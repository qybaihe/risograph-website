# Visual System

## Design Intent

Create a modern Risograph interface: retro print, handmade, tactile, limited spot colors, slightly imperfect registration, and still clearly usable as a contemporary product. The site should feel like a well-designed zine, poster, or indie software launch page translated into UI, not like a dusty antique page.

## Palette

Use these as starting tokens, adapting to the target brand when needed:

- `--riso-bg: #f8f0dc`
- `--riso-paper: #fff8e7`
- `--riso-ink: #172033`
- `--riso-muted: #625b52`
- `--riso-line: #101522`
- `--riso-red: #f05242`
- `--riso-vermilion: #ff6a3d`
- `--riso-yellow: #f6c945`
- `--riso-blue: #4c83c3`
- `--riso-indigo: #27396b`
- `--riso-violet: #7b5aa7`
- `--riso-pink: #ef7b8f`
- `--riso-shadow: rgba(23, 32, 51, 0.18)`

Color balance:

- Warm paper and readable text: 50%-65%.
- Spot-color fields: 25%-35%.
- Halftone texture, registration offsets, print marks: 5%-15%.

Risograph should feel limited and intentional. Prefer two or three dominant spot colors per page, with one supporting accent.

## Surfaces

- Use warm paper backgrounds and off-white content panels.
- Add subtle paper fiber or halftone overlays only at low opacity.
- Use chunky dark outlines, slightly uneven borders, and offset color shadows.
- Create overprint moments by layering translucent red/blue/yellow shapes behind hero objects, cards, or headings.
- Avoid glassmorphism, polished neon gradients, generic SaaS gray cards, and photorealistic paper mockups.

## Typography

- Keep body text modern, readable, and compact.
- Use bold condensed or editorial-feeling headings when the project permits, but keep controls and forms plain.
- Do not apply grain, distortion, rotation, or decorative treatment to long text.
- Avoid negative letter spacing.

## Layout

- Preserve the target site's workflow density and hierarchy.
- Use poster bands, large block headlines, registration marks, stamp-like labels, print frames, and asymmetric image/icon placements to create rhythm.
- Do not wrap every section in a floating card.
- For operational tools, keep the canvas restrained: paper base, clear tables, strong focus rings, and small Riso accents.

## Motifs

Good motifs:

- Halftone dot fields clipped to shapes.
- Misregistered red/blue/yellow offset shadows.
- Rough ink borders and stamp badges.
- Cut-paper panels and torn-edge dividers used sparingly.
- Bundled Risograph icons as stickers or semantic feature art.
- Small print marks, crop marks, numbered poster labels, and overprint circles.

Avoid:

- Old parchment fantasy styling.
- Brown/sepia-only palettes.
- Texture behind small text.
- Low contrast colored text.
- Random confetti or dots on every card.
- Making the site look like a scanned poster instead of an interactive product.
